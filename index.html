01 / ABOUT

Curiosity, translated
into working systems.

My work is driven by a simple question: how can we use electromagnetic waves to solve meaningful engineering and scientific problems?

I am an applied RF and Electromagnetics Scientist with more than 11 years of experience spanning academic research, engineering development and higher education. My work covers antenna design, RF and microwave systems, electromagnetic modelling, wireless technologies and sensing applications.

I currently work as an RF and Microwave Design Engineer at the Infection Innovation Consortium (iiCON), within the Liverpool School of Tropical Medicine, developing microwave sensing systems for next-generation healthcare diagnostics and monitoring.

My doctoral research at the University of Liverpool, conducted in collaboration with Huawei Technologies, focused on liquid antennas for mobile terminal applications—exploring reconfigurability, passive beam steering and MIMO diversity.
